About the project

The distribution problem, investigated

Issuing tokenized assets is largely solved. Distributing them — moving capital from where it sits to where the asset lives, without friction or borders — is the harder, unsolved challenge. Mantle Seeker is a research agent built to attack exactly that.

Project story

Research before distribution

Mantle Seeker doesn't generate instant answers. It investigates first — gathering evidence through Mantle Skills, reasoning over it, scoring its own confidence, and only then producing a Distribution Intelligence Dossier.

Every recommendation explains why, references its supporting evidence, and states when more research is required. Trust is valued over confidence.

Technology
  • Semantic HTML, modern CSS, vanilla JavaScript — no heavy frameworks
  • Animated canvas circuit + particle field for the cyber-intelligence feel
  • Netlify Functions to keep AI keys server-side and secure
  • Transparent, input-derived scoring — no fabricated statistics
  • Accessible, responsive, and print/PDF ready
