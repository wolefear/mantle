AI research workflow

Evidence first. Recommendations second.

Every conclusion carries its reasoning, its supporting evidence, and a confidence level. When evidence is thin, Mantle Seeker says so instead of guessing.

  • Determine the research objective before acting
  • Select only the Mantle Skills the question requires
  • Collect and evaluate evidence, then identify gaps
  • Measure confidence from evidence sufficiency
  • Clearly separate evidence from AI reasoning
